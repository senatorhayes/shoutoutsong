How much does a custom song cost?

How long does it take?

About a minute. Shoutout Song writes the lyrics, then sings and produces the track while you wait.

What do I get?

A full-length MP3 you can download and play anywhere, plus a share link you can text or post.

Is every song original?

Yes. Lyrics are written fresh from the details you enter, and the music is generated new each time. If you don't love it, you can generate another version before buying.

How is the song made?

Shoutout Song uses AI to write the lyrics from your details and to sing and produce the music.

Who is Shoutout Song for?

Parents, grandparents, friends and coworkers who want a fast, personal gift: kids' birthday songs, songs about a child's favorite things, anniversary songs, roasts, retirement send-offs and more.

Can I make a song for a child?

Yes. Use the Kids song form: enter the child's first name, what they love, the occasion and a voice. Kids songs are upbeat, family-friendly sing-alongs.

What if I don't like the song?

Listen to the free preview first. If it's not right, use "Try another" to generate a new version before you buy. See the refund policy for purchased songs.

Can I use the song in a video?

Yes, for personal use: party slideshows, family videos and posts. See the terms for details.

Are songs copied from real artists or shows?

No. Songs are original AI-generated works. They don't copy existing songs or use characters from movies or shows.
